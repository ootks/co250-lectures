About this table

Every cell is editable: change a food's name, its price, or any nutrient value and the diet re-solves automatically. Use + Add food to append a new row. The constraint nutrients are Calories, Fat (g), Protein (g), Carbohydrate (g), Sugars (g), Fiber (g), Cholesterol (mg), and Saturated Fats (g); the cost is Price. Amounts are per 100 g servings.
